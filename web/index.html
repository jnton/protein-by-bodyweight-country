    Interpret the overlays correctly

    Not every number answers the same question.

    Public health

    U.S. guidance now uses 1.2–1.6 g/kg/day

    The 2025–2030 Dietary Guidelines provide this serving goal while the older DRI RDA of 0.8 g/kg/day remains a separate adequacy reference.

    Exercise

    1.4–2.0 g/kg/day for most exercising people

    The ISSN position stand frames this as sufficient for building and maintaining muscle in most exercising individuals.

    Energy restriction

    2.3–3.1 g/kg fat-free mass/day is specialized

    This range was developed for lean, resistance-trained people under hypocaloric conditions. The explorer converts it to a bodyweight equivalent using the body-fat setting.

    Built to be challenged

    The assumptions stay visible.

    01

    Protein is supply

    FAOSTAT reports food available at the end of the supply chain. It is not a direct survey of what each person consumed.

    02

    Weight is estimated

    The preview derives an adult proxy from sex-specific BMI and representative-cohort height. The next model will use age-sex population weights.

    03

    Benchmarks are contextual

    A country being above or below a band does not establish adequacy, deficiency, excess, or an individual recommendation.

    Read the full methodology, limitations, and planned demographic model in the repository.
