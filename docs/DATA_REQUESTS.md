# Data access requests

Potentially valuable sources are not excluded merely because they are currently inaccessible. This file tracks materials that the project owner may be able to obtain.

## Highest-priority files

1. **NCD-RisC 2026 age-specific BMI estimates**
   - Country, calendar year, single year or five-year age group, sex.
   - Mean BMI and uncertainty draws or intervals.
   - Codebook and licence/terms.

2. **NCD-RisC age-specific height estimates**
   - Ages 5–19 by country, year, age, and sex.
   - Adult height estimates suitable for mapping birth cohorts to calendar-year age groups.
   - Uncertainty estimates.

3. **UN World Population Prospects 2024 single-age files**
   - Country/area, year, single age, sex, population.
   - Entity mapping and notes for historical border changes.

4. **Global Dietary Database protein files**
   - Total protein intake estimates.
   - Age, sex, urban/rural, education, year, country.
   - Uncertainty intervals or draws.
   - Full data-use and redistribution terms.

## Additional valuable sources

- Animal and plant protein intake files.
- FAO/WHO GIFT microdata or harmonized indicators.
- National dietary surveys with measured bodyweight.
- Subnational anthropometry by administrative region.
- Papers or supplements that publish national mean body mass.
- Crosswalks between survey regions and current ISO-3166 entities.

## What to provide

A source is most useful when accompanied by:

- original file rather than screenshots;
- codebook/data dictionary;
- paper or technical report;
- licence or terms-of-use page;
- citation requested by the producer;
- notes about whether redistribution is allowed.

Restricted files will remain outside Git and be processed through documented local-only adapters.
